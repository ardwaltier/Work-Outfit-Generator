How recommendations work
Weather wins: choose Today or Tomorrow; the selected day’s temperature, feels-like temperature, forecast high and precipitation are the strongest inputs. Sweaters are excluded at 65°F+ effective temperature, and the L.L.Bean Waterfowl sweater is reserved mostly for sub-50°F days. Dark-brown cords are primarily October–March and are excluded in warmer weather. Rugby shirts are allowed when the forecast high is 72°F or lower. Your 28″ ABC pants are polo-first and Jordan-only—boots and Stan Smiths are excluded with them. Boots are completely excluded from May through September, and from October through April they are only considered when the effective temperature is below 65°F. The picker favors contrast (dark pants with lighter tops, khaki with darker tops), avoids overly brown combinations without separation, treats black dress Blundstones as the more polished boot and Tobacco #1911s as the more casual/rugged boot, and keeps jeans in casual-work territory. The calendar month then nudges color, texture and footwear without overriding unusual weather. Every wardrobe item is included in the outfit engine; curated combinations are preferred, and compatible fallback combinations are generated automatically so Build Around an Item works for every piece.
